🏆 The $20 Expert Challenge

Official rules. Please read all of them. By sending a claim you agree to these rules.

ONE prize of US$20, for the FIRST verified player only.

Finish all 100 stages of any one Remix Arcade game on EXPERT difficulty, in one run, with no cheating, and be the first person whose claim passes the checks below. Free to enter. No purchase needed.

⚠️ Be warned: Expert is built to be extremely hard. From stage 4 every Expert stage has a strict clock that gets shorter each stage; from stage 15 onward the clock is only about 3 seconds per stage. It may not be possible for any human to finish. Nobody has finished it so far. Enter only for the fun of trying.

1. Who runs it

  1. The Challenge is run by the owner of Remix Arcade (“the Organiser”). Contact: appstreamsupportav@gmail.com.
  2. Google, Apple, GitHub and any app store are not sponsors and are not involved in any way.

2. Dates

  1. The Challenge starts when this page is first published and ends at 23:59 South African time (SAST) on 31 December 2026, or as soon as one winner has been verified, whichever comes first.
  2. When the prize has been won, this page will say so. After that, no more prizes are paid to anyone.

3. Who can enter

  1. You must be 18 or older. Under 18s may play for fun but cannot win the prize.
  2. One person, one claim. Teams, shared accounts and claims made for someone else are not allowed.
  3. The Organiser, the Organiser’s family members, and anyone who helped build or test the games cannot win.
  4. The prize cannot be given where the law of your country does not allow this kind of contest.

4. What counts as a win

  1. Pick one Remix Arcade game. Start a NEW GAME on EXPERT and play every stage from 1 to 100 until the stage-100 ending screen appears.
  2. Play only on the official games at verkerkand-cyber.github.io/remix-games or the official Remix Arcade app, in the normal way, on one device, by your own hand.
  3. Paid shop items are not needed and are not required to enter or win.
  4. Not allowed (any of these means you are disqualified): bots, scripts, auto-clickers, macros, other people playing for you, editing or copying saved progress, changing the game code, browser developer tools, speed or time changes, emulators set to slow the game, using any bug or glitch to skip or clear stages, or faking the finish message or claim form.
  5. Continuing a saved Expert run is allowed only if the whole run, from stage 1 to stage 100, is in your recording (rule 5).

5. Proof you must send

  1. A full, unedited screen recording of the whole run: from pressing NEW GAME on Expert, through every stage, to the stage-100 ending showing your claim code. No cuts, no speed-ups. Breaks are allowed only if the recording shows the game paused and then resumed with the same run.
  2. Send by email to appstreamsupportav@gmail.com with the subject “EXPERT CHALLENGE CLAIM”: a link to the recording (e.g. YouTube unlisted or Google Drive), the game name, your claim code, your full name, your country and your phone number.
  3. You must also submit the in-game claim form at the end of the run.
  4. Live check: the Organiser may ask you to play stages of the same game on Expert live on a video call, on a device and at a time agreed with you, chosen within 14 days of your claim. If you cannot reasonably show the same level of skill, or refuse the live check, the claim fails.
  5. The Organiser may compare your recording with the game’s own records (claim code, time and score sent at the finish). If they don’t match, the claim fails.

6. Who is “first”

  1. “First” means the claim email that arrives earliest (by the time shown in the Organiser’s inbox) and passes every check in rules 4 and 5.
  2. Claims are checked one at a time, oldest first. If the earliest claim fails, the next oldest is checked, and so on.
  3. Only one prize of US$20 is paid, ever. Nobody else gets anything, even if they finished at almost the same time.

7. The prize and payment

  1. The prize is US$20, or the same value in South African rand at the bank rate on the payment day. It cannot be swapped for anything else and cannot be transferred.
  2. Before payment the winner must show ID proving their name and age, and the payment account must be in the winner’s own name.
  3. The prize is paid within 30 days after the claim is verified, by bank transfer or another method agreed with the winner. Any bank or transfer fees on the winner’s side, and any taxes, are the winner’s responsibility.
  4. No entry fee is charged and the Organiser will never ask you for money, passwords or card details. Anyone who does is not us.

8. Fair play and decisions

  1. The Organiser checks claims fairly and in good faith, and will give the reason if a claim is refused.
  2. If there is clear evidence of cheating, a bug being used, or a false claim, the claim is disqualified, even after it was first accepted, and any prize paid must be returned.
  3. If the games are hacked, changed by someone else, or break in a way that makes the Challenge unfair, the Organiser may pause or end the Challenge by posting a notice on this page. Claims fully verified before that notice are still honoured.
  4. Game updates may happen during the Challenge. A run must be played on the official version that is live at the time.

9. Your information and publicity

  1. Your details are used only to check your claim and pay the prize, and are kept as records for 3 years. They are never sold or shared except where the law requires.
  2. The winner’s nickname and game may be announced on this page only if the winner agrees. Your recording is only used to check your claim, unless you give permission to share it.

10. Other

  1. These rules are available free of charge on this page at all times. If anything in these rules is unclear, the Organiser’s reasonable interpretation applies, and you can ask by email before you play.
  2. These rules follow the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Nothing in them removes any right you have under South African consumer law.
